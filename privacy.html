This page explains how Stellarminds handles information in plain language. Please have it reviewed by your legal adviser before launch.

Who we are

Stellarminds is an education and skill-development company based in Karur, Tamil Nadu, India. This policy covers this website and the enquiries you send us through it. You can reach us at connect@stellarminds.in or on WhatsApp at +91 96296 64310.

Information we collect

When you use the interest form, you may provide your name, designation, organisation, organisation type, email address, phone number, areas of interest and a message.

The form does not send or store these details on our website or servers. When you submit it, your browser opens WhatsApp with your details written into a message. Your details reach Stellarminds only if you choose to send that message.

If you contact us directly by WhatsApp, phone, email or social media, we receive the details you choose to share in that conversation.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss the programs or partnerships you asked about.
  • To plan and coordinate training sessions with your institution.
  • To keep a record of our conversations so we can follow up on your request.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Sharing your information

We share your information only with the people at Stellarminds who need it to respond to you, and with service providers who help us communicate (for example, WhatsApp and email providers). Messages you send through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Photos and videos

Our gallery shows photos and videos from sessions held at partner institutions. If you appear in an image and would like it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not set its own cookies or use tracking analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit. Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram and partner college websites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry conversations only as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting program, and then delete them unless we need to keep them for legal or accounting reasons.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you have shared with us at any time by writing to connect@stellarminds.in.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
